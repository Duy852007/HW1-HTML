Gunpla

Discover the world of Gunpla

Gunpla is more than just toys; it's the art of model building. Below are some of the outstanding model lines:

High Grade (HG)

1/144 scale, suitable for beginners.

Master Grade (MG)

1/100 scale, high detail and a flexible inner frame.

Perfect Grade (PG)

1/60 scale, the pinnacle of Gunpla molding technology.
