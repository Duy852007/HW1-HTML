Bandai

Globally, there are countless famous toy brands, but when it comes to creativity and the art of crafting intricate models, Japan holds a unique signature, most notably through Bandai. As one of the country's leading entertainment and toy companies, they produce character models and collectibles with an astonishing level of detail. The experience of piecing them together not only keeps me fascinated but also serves as the perfect way to unwind after a long walk.

Speaking of Bandai, it is impossible not to mention their absolute best-selling and most iconic product line: Gunpla (Gundam Plastic Models). With hundreds of millions of units sold worldwide since its debut, Gunpla has become a true symbol of Japanese pop culture. The incredible variety of grades, ranging from beginner-friendly kits to staggeringly complex mechs, is exactly what keeps me thrilled every time I open a new box to build.
